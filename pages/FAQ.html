SnapPress

What is SnapPress?

SnapPress compresses images while preserving their visual quality.

Does SnapPress work offline?

Yes. Every image is processed locally on your computer.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Your files never leave your device.

Which formats does SnapPress support?

SnapPress support: JPG, PNG, AVIF and WebP

Does SnapPress remove EXIF metadata?

Yes. By default SnapPress remove EXIF metadata. However you can enable EXIF metadata preservation, now supported in all supported formats

Can I use SnapPress on mobile?

No. You can't use SnapPress on mobile. Currently SnapPress is available only on Windows (native), Linux and macOS

How often are updates released?

SnapPress offer major release at least once a week.

SnapRename

What is SnapRename?

SnapRename allows you to rename hundreds of files in seconds.

Can I rename multiple files?

Yes. Batch renaming is one of its main features.
