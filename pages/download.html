System Requirements

Minimum Requirements

  • CPU: Any onecore CPU 1GHz+
  • RAM: 256 MB
  • Storage: 100 MB free space
  • Display: at least 1280x720 resolution

Recommended

  • CPU: Any multicore CPU
  • RAM: 512 MB or more
  • Storage: SSD drive
  • Display: 1920x1080 or higher
